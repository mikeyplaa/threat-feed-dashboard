How threats are scored

Each feed scores its own items from 0 to 100. The page then applies three rules that work the same way for every feed:

  • Freshness: after the first 24 hours, an item loses 2 points per day, up to a maximum of −30. Items from lists of currently active threats (OpenPhish, Blocklist.de, DShield, CrowdSec) have no timestamp and are not penalised.
  • Corroboration: when the same CVE, IP or URL appears in more than one feed, the entries are merged and get +5 points per extra feed, up to +10.
  • Diversity: no single feed can take more than 10 of the 25 places.
